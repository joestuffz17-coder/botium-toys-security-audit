# Botium Toys – Compliance Assessment

## 1. Introduction

This document evaluates Botium Toys' compliance posture against selected requirements and principles associated with SOC 1/SOC 2, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS).

## 2. SOC 1 / SOC 2 Assessment

SOC reports provide assurance about controls at service organizations. This assessment considers selected security and data-protection principles.

Key areas evaluated:
- Confidentiality and privacy of sensitive information.
- Availability of data to authorized individuals.
- Integrity, accuracy, and completeness of data.
- Policies governing user access.

**Finding:** Botium Toys needs stronger access management and data-protection practices to support these objectives.

## 3. GDPR Assessment

The GDPR establishes requirements for protecting the personal data of individuals in the European Union.

Key areas evaluated:
- Accurate classification and cataloging of personal data.
- Documented privacy and data-protection procedures.
- Appropriate breach notification procedures.
- Protection of EU customers' personal information.

**Finding:** Botium Toys needs to improve its data classification, privacy protections, and safeguards for customer information.

## 4. PCI DSS Assessment

PCI DSS establishes security requirements for organizations that store, process, or transmit payment card information.

Key areas evaluated:
- Strong password and access-management policies.
- Restricting payment-card data access to authorized personnel.
- Maintaining a secure environment for payment processing.
- Encrypting sensitive payment information.

**Finding:** Botium Toys has significant gaps in protecting payment-card information and controlling access to sensitive data.

## 5. Overall Compliance Summary

The assessment identified weaknesses in data protection, access management, and payment-card security.

Botium Toys should strengthen its security controls and review its procedures against applicable compliance requirements.

## 6. Conclusion

Improving its compliance posture will help Botium Toys protect customer information, reduce regulatory risks, and establish stronger security practices.
