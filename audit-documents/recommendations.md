# Botium Toys – Security Recommendations

## 1. Introduction

Based on the internal security audit, several weaknesses were identified in Botium Toys' security controls. The following recommendations are intended to reduce security risks, protect sensitive information, and improve business operations.

## 2. Recommended Security Improvements

### 1. Implement the Principle of Least Privilege
Restrict employee access to systems and information based on their job responsibilities. Employees should only have access to the resources necessary to perform their duties.

### 2. Strengthen Password Security
Establish stronger password requirements and implement a centralized password management system. Encourage multi-factor authentication (MFA) where appropriate.

### 3. Encrypt Sensitive Information
Encrypt customer personal information and payment-card data, both when stored and when transmitted, to reduce the risk of unauthorized access.

### 4. Establish Disaster Recovery Procedures
Develop a disaster recovery plan and perform regular backups of critical business data. Test recovery procedures to ensure business continuity.

### 5. Implement an Intrusion Detection System
Deploy an Intrusion Detection System (IDS) to monitor network activity and identify suspicious behavior or potential security incidents.

### 6. Improve Access Management
Introduce clear user access policies and separation of duties. Review employee permissions regularly and remove unnecessary access.

### 7. Improve Compliance Practices
Review security procedures against applicable GDPR and PCI DSS requirements. Maintain documentation and periodically evaluate the effectiveness of security controls.

### 8. Maintain Physical Security
Continue using physical safeguards such as locks, CCTV surveillance, and fire detection systems. Inspect and maintain these controls regularly.

## 3. Priority Areas

The most urgent improvements should focus on:

1. Protecting sensitive customer and payment information.
2. Restricting unauthorized access to company systems.
3. Establishing reliable backups and disaster recovery procedures.
4. Improving monitoring and detection capabilities.

## 4. Conclusion

Implementing these recommendations would strengthen Botium Toys' overall security posture, reduce potential vulnerabilities, improve protection of customer information, and support business continuity.
